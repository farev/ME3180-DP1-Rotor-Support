RReview verdict

Rev P3 passes every Rev2 requirement with the Piazza-approved shaft properties. The geometry is the P2 design; only the shaft stock changed, after the approved 1045 yield strength (75 ksi) came in below the 598 MPa we had proposed and the cost rule was read as the full price of the shortest sufficient stock. Items marked ⚠ are corrections or improvements for the report.

Verified

Arithmetic. Reactions, moments, Marin factors, notch sensitivity, all five section checks, the key, screw, pin and ring checks and the BOM total were rebuilt from scratch with vector statics (r × F about each cut). Every value matches the provisional PDF to four decimals.

Verified

Groove Kt = 3.6 at the worst corner. Reading Shigley Fig. A-15-16 (11th ed., p. 1047) at a/t = 1.41 for root Ø1.456 in and R 0.008 in: the r/t = 0.20 curve gives 4.23 and r/t = 0.40 gives 3.43, so r/t = 0.364 gives Kt ≈ 3.58, read as 3.6. Shigley's Example 7-2 reads this chart the same way (Kt = 4.3 at r/t = 0.208, a/t = 1.42) with no added margin.

⚠ Cost

Shaft choice (Rev P3). A solver (DP2/solver) ran every McMaster rotary shaft and every stainless ring for its diameter through these same equations. 4138N27 (1-11/16 in 1045, $202.18) is the cheapest that passes fatigue and Shigley yield. 1346K534 (1566, HRC25, 1-1/2 × 24 in, $222.07) costs $19.89 more and is selected for its slightly larger fatigue margin (1.43 against 1.38), milder groove and standard 1-1/2 in rings, key and pin; yield margins are about equal (3.91 against 3.92). See Step 10.

Method

Yield uses Shigley Eq. (7-15), with Kf. With Kf = 2.73 at the groove, ny = 3.92 governs (needs Sy ≥ 465 MPa); the keyseat follows at 5.61. The Kt elastic peak at the worst-case groove gives 2.97 (it would need 612 MPa); it is shown for information and is not a Rev2 requirement.

Fixed

Hardness conversion. Shigley 11th ed. Eq. (2-36) is Sut = 3.4 HB MPa. For the 1566 shaft, HRC25 = HB 253, so Sut = 3.4(253) = 860.2 MPa. Piazza lists only Sy, so this hardness minimum is used.

Fixed

Groove callout relaxed. R 0.0098–0.0100 in was a 5 µm band that could not be cut or inspected. Rev P3 calls out root Ø1.456–1.460 in and R 0.008–0.010 in (0.20–0.25 mm), cut with a standard corner-radius grooving insert and within Smalley's 0.010 in limit. The checks use the worst corner: deepest root, smallest radius.

Changed P2

Key and keyseat to ASME B17.1. The class rule says a key must be longer than it is wide, so the key is 1/2 in (12.70 h14). McMaster's key stock has square edges, so the cut key gets the B17.1 3/64 in × 45° chamfer to clear the 1/32 in keyseat fillet. Key factors: 26.7 in shear, 15.2 in bearing. Slot end-centre distance 12.70 H14, overall 22.225 mm.

Fixed

Set-screw torque from the load. McMaster lists no torque for 94355A533, and Unbrako's 70 in·lb applies to stronger ASTM F880 screws. The torque is now the smallest that holds the 35 N sprocket with n = 1.2 at the low end of the ±25% preload scatter: 0.24 N·m, with K = 0.2 and μ = 0.15 from Shigley. The screw then has n = 4.74 at the thread root.

Verified

Notch sensitivity. q = 0.666 at r = 0.008 in (Shigley Eqs. 6-33, 6-35, checked in US and SI forms). Fatigue still passes with q up to 0.87. With Kf = Kt everywhere (as the reference design does), only a 1-15/16 in shaft passes, at $306.55.

Resolved

Shaft properties. Sy = 88 ksi for 1566 at HRC25, D ≤ 1.5 in, is on the instructor's approved list (10 Nov). No request is needed.

Chain force components

The chain pulls with 161.8 N at θ = −4.732° from x, measured about y. A rotation θ about +y takes x̂ to (cos θ, 0, −sin θ):

\[F_{c,x}=F_c\cos\theta=161.8\cos(-4.732^\circ)=161.25\ \text{N}\]
(1)
\[F_{c,z}=-F_c\sin\theta=-161.8\sin(-4.732^\circ)=+13.35\ \text{N}\]
(2)

The +z component adds to the drag, which is the conservative sign. The weights act along the axis: rotor 120 N (through the pin), sprocket 35 N (through key and set screw) and shaft self-weight:

\[W_{sh}=\rho g\frac{\pi D^2}{4}L=7850(9.807)\frac{\pi(0.0381)^2}{4}(0.554)=48.62\ \text{N}\]
(3)
\[N=W_r+W_s+W_{sh}=120+35+48.62=203.62\ \text{N}\]
(4)

Using the full Ø38.1 cylinder slightly overestimates the weight of the machined shaft.

Hub model check

A statically consistent hub contact (−1820 N at y = −160, +2380 N at y = −240) sums to 560 N with moment −280 N·m about y = 0. The pin-hole check instead uses the larger cantilever value at the hole edge (181 N·m). Average contact pressure in a 10 mm band: 2380/(38.00 × 10) = 6.26 MPa. It is radial compression, so, as in Shigley, it is not added to the axial shaft stresses.

05Where the highest stress is (Shaft item 2)

The shaft has one diameter and no shoulders, so the only stress raisers are the two ring grooves, the keyseat and the pin hole. The largest nominal moment is at the lower bearing midplane (284.5 N·m), but there is no notch there. Half a bearing width lower, the lower retaining-ring groove combines almost the same moment (280.5 N·m) with the sharpest notch (Kt 3.5) and the smallest section (root Ø37.06 mm). It carries no torque.

The keyseat upper end comes second: slightly less moment (266.4 N·m), a milder notch, and the full 19.63 N·m torque. The pin hole sees much less moment (181 N·m). The upper groove sees no bending or torque.

Result (Shaft items 2–3)

Highest stress: lower ring groove, M = 280.5 N·m, T = 0, with N = 203.6 N axial tension. Second: keyseat upper end, M = 266.4 N·m, T = 19.63 N·m. Step 9 confirms the ranking: the groove governs both yield and fatigue.

11Key (Hardware item 1)

D918-8 stainless square key, 3/8 × 3/8 in, cut 1/2 in long (12.70 ± 0.05 mm) from 36 in stock 92530A319 (width and height −0.003/0 in). The class rule requires a key longer than it is wide: 12.70 > 9.525 mm. Key spans y = −42.35 to −29.65, inside the un-toothed sprocket hub (y = −46.39 to −25.03), centred under the set screw.

D10Closed keyseat, ASME B17.1 Class 1: width 9.525 +0.051/−0 mm (3/8 in +0.002/−0); depth 3/16 in at the side wall, dimensioned S = D − Y − H/2 = 1.5000 − 0.0238 − 0.1875 = 1.2887 in +0.000/−0.015 (Y = chordal height); floor fillet R 1/32 in. B17.1 gives no length tolerance, so the length uses ISO 286 IT14: key 12.70 h14 (+0/−0.43), slot end-centre distance 12.70 H14 (+0.43/−0).
